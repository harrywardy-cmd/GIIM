# GIIM for managers

GIIM is IT's system for equipment and for getting new starters ready. As a manager you use it to **approve device
requests** for your team, **raise a request** when someone needs a device, and **see what your team has**. Open it from
the **GIIM tile in My Apps** (myapps.microsoft.com); there's no separate password.

## Approving a request

When a device is requested for someone in your team, you get an email with a link. In GIIM, the **bell** at the top
shows requests waiting for you (and any question an approver has asked about a request you raised), as does
**Approvals**.

On the request, choose:

- **Approve**: IT then hands over a device from stock or orders one. You can add a budget code.
- **Reject**: give a reason; the person who asked is told.
- **Request info**: ask a question. The request waits until it's answered, and you're emailed when it is.

If a request waits more than 2 days, you get a reminder (every 2 days, three at most). Requests for a new starter are
raised automatically from the starter's ticket, so approve them before the start date, or IT can't hand over a device.

You approve requests for people who report to you in the staff directory. Nobody can approve their own request. If the
directory has the wrong manager for someone, tell the service desk.

## Raising a request

**Requests → New request**: who it's for, what kind of device, why, and when it's needed. If it's for someone in your
own team, raising it counts as your approval. You're emailed when it's approved or rejected and when the device is
handed over; **Requests → My requests** shows where each one is up to.

## New starters

When HR's starter ticket reaches the service desk, GIIM emails you within a few minutes: the start date, the devices
IT is arranging (and which need your approval), and the apps and access being set up. Open the link to see the
checklist as IT works through it.

## Leavers

When someone in your team is leaving, you're emailed the list of equipment they hold, so it can come back to IT by
their last day. If anything is still out after that, you get a reminder the next day, then weekly (three at most).
Devices reported lost or stolen aren't included.

## Seeing what your team has

Search for a person in the top bar, or open **People**: their profile shows the devices they hold now, what they've
held before, and their device requests. **Reports** has the full lists, with Excel export.

## Questions

Raise a ticket with the service desk as usual. GIIM signs you out after 8 hours unused, or 10 hours after you signed
in; click the GIIM tile again to carry on.
